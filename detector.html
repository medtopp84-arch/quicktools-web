📌 What Are AI Text Detectors?

AI text detectors are tools that analyze text to determine if it was written by a human or generated by artificial intelligence like ChatGPT, Claude, or GPT-4. These tools are essential for educators, content creators, and professionals who need to verify the authenticity of written content.

💡 Tips for Using AI Detectors

  • 🔹 Test multiple tools – Different detectors may give slightly different results
  • 🔹 Use longer text – AI detection is more accurate with longer text (200+ words)
  • 🔹 Check for false positives – Some human-written text may be flagged as AI
  • 🔹 Combine with plagiarism checkers – For complete content verification
